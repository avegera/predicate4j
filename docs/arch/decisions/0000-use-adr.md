## Context

We will use ADRs. The main question was which template to adopt.

## Decision Drivers

- Structured, option-based reasoning
- Clarity and maintainability
- Simplicity and wide adoption

## Considered Options

- [MADR 4.0.0](https://adr.github.io/madr/)
- [Michael's Nygard template](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions)
- Other templates (from [Joel Parker Henderson’s list](https://github.com/joelparkerhenderson/architecture-decision-record/))
- No convention

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: MADR 4.0.0. It is lean, structured, widely known, and supports option-based evaluation.
